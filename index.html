Privacy Policy

App: Nyxar: Dream Journal

Developer: DreamLexicon

Effective date: April 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nyxar: Dream Journal handles information. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Nyxar: Dream Journal stores dream entries and related app data locally on your device.

Locally stored data may include:
  • Dream entries you write
  • Dream titles you optionally add
  • Dream mood and intensity selections
  • Favorites, profile preferences, language selection, and app settings
  • Local progress systems such as energy / reward counters

2. Data Storage

Your core dream journal data is stored locally on your device using local app storage. The developer does not operate a backend server for storing your dream journal data.

3. Advertising

The app may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. These services may collect limited device-related information, advertising identifiers, and usage data to provide and measure ads, according to their own policies.

4. Purchases and Rewards

If the app includes rewarded ad features or optional purchase-related UI, these systems are used to unlock in-app value such as energy, rewards, or ad-free usage. Purchase processing, if enabled in the future, will be handled by the relevant app store provider.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app may send local notifications on your device. Notification preferences are stored locally and can be changed inside the app or your device settings.

6. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell your dream journal data. Core journal entries are not shared with the developer through a dedicated backend server.

7. Your Choices

  • You can delete your dream entries from inside the app.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data from your device, subject to your device behavior.
  • You can manage ad and notification permissions through your device settings.

8. Children’s Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through a dedicated backend service. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided sensitive data should delete the data from the device.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page by revising the effective date.

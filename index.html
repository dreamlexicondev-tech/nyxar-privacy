Privacy Policy — Nyxar: Dream Journal

Last updated: October 3, 2026

DreamLexicon provides Nyxar: Dream Journal (com.dreamlexicon.nyxar). Contact: DreamLexicon Support.

Your local journal

Nyxar stores dream entries, titles, dates, tags, mood/intensity selections, drafts, saved reflections or interpretation text, favorites, summaries and local profile preferences on your device. The profile can include a display name, status and avatar you choose. It is a local profile, not a server account. Language, appearance and preview preferences are also stored locally.

The current journal does not provide cloud synchronization or send your dream text to a remote interpretation or advertising service. The atlas and its scene use local journal data. Local reflection tools are not a medical diagnosis or a prediction service.

Local protection and previews

On Android, the protected journal, draft, summary and profile components use AES-GCM encryption with a device-bound key managed by Android Keystore. Older readable records are migrated before the old copy is removed. This does not make every exported file encrypted. Loss of the device key or removing app data can make protected records inaccessible; keep an export you can read before removing the app or changing devices. Android app backup and device transfer are disabled for this app.

The optional hide-preview setting restricts screenshots and recent-app previews on supported Android devices. That setting is separate from storage encryption and does not protect a file you share.

Advertising and privacy choices

This app uses Google AdMob. Its advertising services can process IP-derived approximate location, advertising/device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse. Our app does not send your saved record contents as ad parameters. Google handles this information under its own policies.

The app uses Google's consent system. Eligible advertising requests follow its consent state. Where required and available, Settings offers an advertising privacy form; Android also provides advertising privacy controls. Choosing a different advertising mode does not necessarily stop all technical data processing. The app's offline features remain available when advertising is unavailable.

Google privacy information: Google Privacy Policy.

Exports, imports and deletion

You can export readable JSON or Markdown, or create a password-protected portable backup. Readable files contain your journal text; anyone you give them to may read them. Protect the password for an encrypted backup; the developer cannot recover it. A device-recovery container is separate and is not a portable password backup.

Export/import uses your selected files and Android sharing. Temporary app-created export files are tracked for cleanup, but copies saved or shared outside the app remain under your control. Deleting journal entries or the journal/draft through the app does not remove those external copies. Android's Clear storage control removes local app data. Some appearance or profile preferences can have separate controls. Advertising data held by Google is separate.

Children and support

Nyxar does not operate a server for collecting children's dream entries. Parents or guardians can use the on-device deletion controls and contact DreamLexicon with a privacy concern. Do not send a private dream journal to support unless it is necessary and you choose to do so.

Support, rights and security

If you contact DreamLexicon, we receive the message, your contact details and any information you choose to send. Use dreamlexicon.sup@gmail.com for access, correction or deletion requests about information held by DreamLexicon. Do not send passwords or unnecessary private records. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

Data held independently by third-party services is governed by their own policies. Those services may process information outside your country. Depending on your location, additional privacy rights and a right to complain to your data-protection authority may apply. No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.
